## ⭐ Featured Projects

### 🤖 Supplier Intelligence Copilot

**Supply Chain · AI Product · Decision Support**

An AI-assisted supplier-risk decision-support prototype that combines **deterministic supply-chain analytics** with AI-based synthesis and recommendations.

The project started as a SQL and Tableau Supply Chain Health Monitor covering **100 SKUs across 5 suppliers**.

I evolved it to answer a more useful operational question:

> **Which supplier needs attention, why, and what should I investigate next?**

The system keeps supplier metrics and risk classification deterministic while using AI to:

* Explain major risk drivers
* Connect conclusions to evidence
* Surface healthy signals alongside risks
* Recommend areas for investigation
* Explicitly identify missing information
* Preserve human review for consequential procurement decisions

**Key Concepts:** Context Engineering · Structured Outputs · Guardrails · Human-in-the-Loop AI · AI Evaluation · Product Thinking

**Tools:** SQL · Tableau · HTML/CSS/JavaScript · OpenAI API · Netlify

🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/parulwalkey/supply-chain-health-monitor)
🔗 [View Live Prototype](https://supplier-intelligence-copilot.netlify.app)
